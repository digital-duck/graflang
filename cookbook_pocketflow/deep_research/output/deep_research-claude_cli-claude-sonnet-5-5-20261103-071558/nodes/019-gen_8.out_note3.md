**Background knowledge (unverified against current sources)**
- **Magic state distillation:** It produces high-fidelity non-Clifford resources (T or CCZ states) from many noisy copies. This is needed because transversal gates on common codes can't give a universal set. Magic state cultivation is a newer, lower-overhead alternative to full distillation factories.
- **Lattice surgery:** It performs logical multi-qubit operations on surface-code-like patches by merging and splitting them. This gives logical CNOT and Pauli-product measurements with only nearest-neighbor interactions.
- **Neutral atoms:** Reconfigurable tweezer arrays can shuttle atoms, which allows high-rate parallel gates and non-local connectivity. They have been used for logical-qubit demonstrations, and their main challenges are atom loss and mid-circuit readout.
- **Trapped ions:** They offer the highest two-qubit fidelities and all-to-all connectivity within a trap. Ion shuttling in QCCD architectures supports transversal gates and small-code logical operations. Gate speed and scaling to many qubits are the main challenges.
