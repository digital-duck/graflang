## 1. Experimental demonstrations by platform

### Superconducting
- **Google Quantum AI (Willow):**
  - Surface code memory at distances 3, 5 and 7 showed below-threshold scaling.
  - Logical error suppression Λ was about 2.1 per distance step.
  - The d=7 logical error rate was about 0.14% per cycle.
  - It was posted in late 2024 and published in Nature in 2025.
- **USTC (Zuchongzhi 3.x):**
  - I recall a distance-7 surface code with below-threshold behavior and a smaller Λ than Google's.
  - I'm unsure of the exact figures.
- **IBM (qLDPC):**
  - The roadmap is built on bivariate bicycle ("gross") codes, with a fault-tolerant system (Starling) targeted for around 2029.
  - Demonstrations so far are of components, such as decoding and real-time syndrome processing.
  - I'm not aware of a full below-threshold qLDPC memory result from IBM.

### Neutral atoms
- Harvard/QuEra and others have reported logical-qubit and fault-tolerant architecture demonstrations.
- Reconfigurable tweezer arrays can shuttle atoms, which gives non-local connectivity and high-rate parallel gates. This suits qLDPC-style codes.
- Main challenges are atom loss and mid-circuit readout.
- I can't confirm specific logical error rates.

### Trapped ions
- They have the highest two-qubit fidelities and all-to-all connectivity within a trap.
- Ion shuttling in QCCD architectures supports transversal gates and small-code logical operations.
- Main challenges are gate speed and scaling to many qubits.

## 2. Decoding

- **Classical decoders:**
  - Minimum-weight perfect matching (MWPM, e.g. PyMatching).
  - Union-find.
  - Belief propagation with ordered statistics (BP+OSD) for qLDPC codes.
- **Neural decoders:**
  - Examples are Google's AlphaQubit (transformer/recurrent) and CNN, graph neural network and transformer decoders trained on simulated or experimental syndromes.
  - They can beat MWPM on accuracy.
  - Latency and scaling to larger distances are open problems.
- **Hardware-accelerated decoders:**
  - FPGA and ASIC implementations, such as the Riverlane and Helios union-find decoders.
  - Real-time decoding needs roughly microsecond latency per round to avoid a growing backlog.
- **Other approaches:**
  - Sliding-window and parallel-window decoding.
  - Local or predecoder stages that pass only hard cases to a global decoder.

## 3. Logical operations and universality

- **Magic state distillation:**
  - It produces high-fidelity non-Clifford resources (T or CCZ states) from many noisy copies.
  - It is needed because transversal gates on common codes can't give a universal gate set.
  - Magic state cultivation is a newer, lower-overhead alternative to full distillation factories.
- **Lattice surgery:**
  - It performs logical multi-qubit operations on surface-code-like patches by merging and splitting them.
  - It gives logical CNOT and Pauli-product measurements with only nearest-neighbor interactions.
