**The capital of France is Paris.**

Paris is on the Seine River in the north of the country. It has been France's capital for most of the past thousand years and is also its largest city, as well as its political, economic, and cultural center. The national government, including the President's residence (the Élysée Palace) and the French Parliament, is based there.
