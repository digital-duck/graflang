# Your Final Recipe: Lemon Garlic Herb Roasted Chicken

This is a great dinner to end up with. Your four ingredients (chicken, garlic, lemon, and fresh herbs) are all you need, and each one has a real job.

**The plan:**
1. **Marinade:** Mix minced garlic (4-5 cloves), chopped herbs, the zest and juice of one lemon, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
2. **Season:** Pat the chicken dry and rub the marinade over and under the skin. Rest it 30 minutes, or overnight in the fridge for deeper flavor.
3. **Arrange:** Put the squeezed lemon halves and a few herb sprigs in the cavity or around the pieces in a roasting pan.
4. **Roast at 425°F (220°C):** About 45-60 minutes for a whole bird, or 30-40 minutes for pieces. It's done when the thickest part reads 165°F (74°C).
5. **Rest and serve:** Give it 10 minutes, spoon the pan juices over the top, and finish with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon.
