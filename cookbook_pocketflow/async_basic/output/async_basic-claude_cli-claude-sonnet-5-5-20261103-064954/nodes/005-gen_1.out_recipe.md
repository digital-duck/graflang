## Lemon Garlic Herb Roasted Chicken

A classic roast that uses all four of your ingredients: garlic and lemon flavor the meat, and fresh herbs go both under the skin and in the pan. The result is juicy chicken with crisp, fragrant skin and a bright citrus finish.

**Key steps**
1. **Prep the marinade:** Mince 4-5 garlic cloves and chop a handful of fresh herbs (thyme, rosemary, parsley). Mix with the zest and juice of one lemon, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
2. **Season the chicken:** Pat the chicken dry. Rub the marinade over the surface and under the skin, and let it rest 30 minutes (or up to overnight in the fridge).
3. **Stuff and arrange:** Put the squeezed lemon halves and a few herb sprigs in the cavity (or scatter them around the pieces, if using parts) in a roasting pan.
4. **Roast:** Bake at 425°F (220°C) for about 45-60 minutes for a whole bird, or 30-40 minutes for pieces, until the thickest part reaches 165°F (74°C).
5. **Rest and serve:** Rest 10 minutes, spoon the pan juices over the top, and finish with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon.
