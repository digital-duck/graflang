# Lemon Garlic Herb Roasted Chicken

Good news: your four ingredients make one of the best classic dinners there is. Here's a roast chicken that uses every one of them.

## How your ingredients are used

- **Chicken:** the star. Use a whole bird or pieces, whichever you have.
- **Garlic:** 4-5 minced cloves go into the marinade and flavor the meat all the way through.
- **Lemon:** you use the zest and juice in the marinade, then tuck the squeezed halves into the cavity or around the pan. The brightness cuts through the richness of the chicken.
- **Fresh herbs:** thyme, rosemary, and parsley work well. They go under the skin and into the pan, so they perfume the meat as it roasts. A few fresh ones on top finish the dish.

## Why it's a great pick

- Every ingredient has a real job, and nothing is left over.
- It needs little effort: mix, rub, roast.
- It gives you juicy chicken, crisp skin, and a bright, fragrant finish.
- The pan juices make a ready-made sauce.

## How to make it

1. **Make the marinade:** Mince the garlic and chop the herbs. Mix with the zest and juice of one lemon, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
2. **Season the chicken:** Pat it dry, then rub the marinade over the surface and under the skin. Let it rest 30 minutes, or up to overnight in the fridge for deeper flavor.
3. **Arrange:** Put the squeezed lemon halves and a few herb sprigs in the cavity, or scatter them around the pieces, in a roasting pan.
4. **Roast at 425°F (220°C):** Allow about 45-60 minutes for a whole bird, or 30-40 minutes for pieces. It's done when the thickest part reads 165°F (74°C).
5. **Rest and serve:** Let it sit 10 minutes, spoon the pan juices over the top, and finish with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon.
