Vector databases are specialized data stores optimized for storing and querying high-dimensional
numerical vectors, called embeddings. They are a foundational component of modern AI applications
that require semantic search, including recommendation engines, question-answering systems,
and RAG pipelines.

Popular vector databases include FAISS, Chroma, Weaviate, Pinecone, and Qdrant. FAISS (Facebook
AI Similarity Search) is an open-source library developed by Meta that provides highly efficient
similarity search over dense vectors. It supports both exact and approximate nearest-neighbor
search, with approximate methods trading a small accuracy cost for dramatically lower latency
at billion-vector scale.

When building a RAG system, the choice of embedding model matters as much as the vector database.
Embedding models such as all-MiniLM-L6-v2, text-embedding-3-small, and BGE-M3 transform text
into fixed-length float vectors that capture semantic meaning. Documents with similar meaning
have vectors that are geometrically close in the embedding space, enabling retrieval by cosine
similarity or L2 distance rather than exact keyword matching.
