Probability is a number between 0 and 1 that tells you how likely something is to happen, where 0 means it can never happen and 1 means it is certain. You can find it by counting: if a coin has two equally likely sides, the chance of heads is 1 out of 2, or 0.5, and if you roll a six-sided die, the chance of any one number is 1 out of 6. It doesn't predict what will happen on a single try, but over many tries the results settle close to those numbers, which is why probability helps us make good guesses about an uncertain world.
