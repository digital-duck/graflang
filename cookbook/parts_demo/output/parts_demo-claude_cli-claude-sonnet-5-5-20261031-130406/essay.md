1. AI will make learning more personal. Intelligent tutoring systems will adjust lessons and pacing to each student's needs and gaps in skills.

2. Universities will need to move away from memorizing facts and standardized tests. They should focus on skills that are distinctly human, like critical thinking, creativity, and solving complex problems, which AI still does poorly.

3. AI tools will take over administrative work and give schools data-based analysis. This will free faculty to spend more time with students and on research.

4. Working with AI will become part of how students learn. They will use it as a capable assistant for research, analysis, and coming up with ideas.

5. In the end, higher education will shift toward teaching adaptability and lifelong learning, so graduates can do well in a world that keeps changing because of AI.
