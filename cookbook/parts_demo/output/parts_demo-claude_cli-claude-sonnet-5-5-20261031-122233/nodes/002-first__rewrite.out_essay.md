AI-assisted education employs instruments such as adaptive tutoring systems and conversational assistants to tailor instruction to each student's pace and requirements. It can provide immediate, patient feedback on writing, programming, and problem-solving that a single teacher could not offer to every learner simultaneously. Educators may delegate routine tasks, such as drafting quizzes and grading assignments, thereby freeing time for mentorship and discussion. Nevertheless, significant risks remain, including inaccurate responses, overreliance that undermines critical thinking, and inequitable access to technology. The most favorable outcomes are achieved when AI supplements skilled educators and is accompanied by clear guidelines that ensure students remain actively engaged in independent thought.
