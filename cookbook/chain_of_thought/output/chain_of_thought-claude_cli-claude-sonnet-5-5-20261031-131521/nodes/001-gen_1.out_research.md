## Root causes (contested, but these are the main strands)
- **Post-Soviet unresolved status:** Ukraine became independent in 1991. Russia's leadership has long denied that Ukraine is a fully separate nation (Putin's 2021 essay "On the Historical Unity of Russians and Ukrainians").
- **Westward drift vs. Russian "sphere of influence":** NATO and EU enlargement, plus Ukraine's 2008 Bucharest "future membership" pledge. Realist scholars (Mearsheimer) call this the main trigger. Others argue Russia's imperial and revanchist aims are the core cause and NATO was a pretext.
- **2014 rupture:** Euromaidan led to Yanukovych's ouster, then Russia annexed Crimea and backed separatists in Donbas. The Minsk agreements (2014/2015) failed to hold.
- **Immediate trigger:** Russia demanded in December 2021 that NATO roll back and bar Ukraine, was refused, and launched the full-scale invasion on 24 Feb 2022.

## Current state (as of my latest knowledge)
- **Front:** A grinding attritional war. Russia holds roughly 19-20% of Ukraine's territory and has made slow, costly gains, mainly in Donetsk oblast. Drones dominate the battlefield and make large breakthroughs rare.
- **Strike war:** Russia hits Ukrainian cities and the energy grid with missiles and drones. Ukraine hits Russian refineries, depots, and airbases deep inside Russia.
- **Diplomacy:** The Trump administration pushed negotiations through 2025 (Alaska summit, a US peace framework). Core disputes remain: territory (Donbas), security guarantees, Ukrainian army size, and sanctions relief. I'm not aware of a durable settlement, so verify whether that has changed.
- **Support:** Europe has taken on more of the financing and arms supply as US direct aid shrank. The EU has debated using frozen Russian central bank assets (about €200bn+) and agreed large loan packages. North Korea has supplied troops and munitions to Russia.
- **Human cost:** Combined military casualties are estimated in the hundreds of thousands to over a million (estimates vary widely). Thousands of civilians are verified killed, and several million Ukrainians are refugees abroad.

## Potential fallout
- **Europe's security:** Rearmament, NATO's 5% GDP spending target, Finland and Sweden joining NATO, and a permanent eastern-flank buildup.
- **Territorial precedent:** A deal that rewards conquest weakens the norm against changing borders by force, which has implications for Taiwan and others.
- **Escalation risks:** Nuclear signaling, hybrid attacks (sabotage, drones, cyber) on NATO states, and the risk of direct NATO-Russia incidents.
- **Economic:** Europe's energy decoupling from Russia, sanctions fragmenting trade, Russia's war-footing economy (inflation, labor shortages, rising reliance on China), and Ukraine's reconstruction bill, which the World Bank puts at roughly $500bn+.
- **Geopolitical:** Deeper Russia-China-Iran-North Korea alignment, Ukraine's EU accession path, and strained transatlantic ties.
- **Demographic and political:** Long-term population loss in both countries, and possible political instability in Russia or Ukraine depending on how the war ends.
