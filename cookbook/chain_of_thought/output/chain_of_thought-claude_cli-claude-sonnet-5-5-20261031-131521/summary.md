# Executive Brief: Russia-Ukraine War

**Data caveat:** Based on knowledge through about June 2026; today is 31 Oct 2026. Diplomacy, front lines, and aid details may be four or more months stale. Verify against ISW, Reuters, or OHCHR.

## Situation
- **Attritional, drone-dominated war.** Breakthroughs are rare. Russia's gains are slow and costly, mostly in Donetsk. Neither side can win decisively, so the conflict points toward a negotiated or frozen end state.
- **Symmetric strike war.** Russia hits cities and the grid. Ukraine hits refineries, depots, and airbases deep in Russia. Both aim to raise the other's economic and political cost.
- **Support is shifting to Europe.** US direct aid has shrunk, and Europe is covering financing and arms. Russia relies on North Korean troops and munitions and on China.
- **Diplomacy is stuck on four issues:** Donbas territory, security guarantees, Ukrainian army size, and sanctions relief. The 2025 Trump-led push did not resolve them.
- **Both economies are strained.** Russia faces inflation and labor shortages. Ukraine's reconstruction bill is roughly $500bn+.

## Implications
- **Any settlement will reflect who can sustain costs longer.** Watch Russian economic strain, European financing durability, and US commitment.
- **Frozen Russian assets (€200bn+):** Using them would fund Ukraine for years but sets a precedent for reserve security and euro-held assets.
- **Spillover risk:** A deal that rewards conquest weakens the norm against changing borders by force (Taiwan and other disputes). Security guarantees matter as much as territorial terms.
- **Europe is rearming for the long term** (NATO 5% target, Finland and Sweden accessions, eastern-flank buildups), however the war ends.
- **Main escalation path:** Sabotage, drones, cyber attacks, and nuclear signaling could produce an accidental direct NATO-Russia incident.
- **The Russia-China-Iran-North Korea alignment is deepening,** which erodes sanctions effectiveness.

## Caveats
- Root causes are contested (realist: NATO enlargement; revanchist: Russian imperial aims). Policy conclusions differ, so this is not settled.
- Casualty estimates range from hundreds of thousands to over a million, too wide for firm conclusions.
- The ~19-20% occupied-territory figure is point-in-time; check a current source.
