Two SaaS firms A and B simultaneously choose a monthly subscription price: High ($100/mo) or Low ($70/mo). If both choose High: each earns $40K/mo profit (40% market share × $100 margin). If both choose Low: each earns $25K/mo profit (50% share × $50 margin). If A chooses High and B chooses Low: A earns $10K (loses share) and B earns $55K. If A chooses Low and B chooses High: A earns $55K and B earns $10K.
