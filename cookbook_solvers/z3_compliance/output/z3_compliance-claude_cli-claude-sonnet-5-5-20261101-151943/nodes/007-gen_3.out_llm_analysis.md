**Variables:** age, income, collateral (bool), self_employed (bool), credit score, months employed.

## 1. Satisfiable?

Yes. Every rule is a constraint that can be met at once. Collateral alone satisfies R2 and R5, so only R1, R3 and R4 need real values.

## 2. Example qualifying customer

- Age: 35
- Annual income: $85,000
- Collateral: yes
- Self-employed: no
- Credit score: 700
- Months employed: 24

Check:
- R1: 35 ≥ 18 ✓
- R2: $85,000 ≥ $30,000 ✓ (collateral also true)
- R3: not self-employed, so the implication holds vacuously ✓
- R4: 700 ≥ 650 ✓
- R5: 24 ≥ 12 ✓

## 3. Hidden interactions and traps

- **Self-employment vs. income (R3 with R2):** A self-employed applicant with $35,000 income and no collateral passes R2 but fails R3, which needs $50,000. R2's $30,000 threshold is meaningless for self-employed people, whose real floor is $50,000, collateral or not. Collateral does not waive R3.
- **Credit tier vs. income (R4):** The 600-649 credit band only works with income ≥ $80,000. A 620-score applicant earning $60,000 fails R4 even though they clear R2 and R3 easily.
- **Collateral is a one-sided escape hatch:** It satisfies R2 and R5 but has no effect on R1, R3 or R4. A customer with collateral and a 590 credit score is still rejected.
- **Self-employed and tenure (R5):** A newly self-employed applicant (under 12 months) must have collateral, because R5 has no other way to pass. Combined with R3, they need $50,000+ income and collateral.
- **Credit floor:** Nothing in R4 can be offset. Below 600, no income level or collateral rescues the applicant.

## 4. Most restrictive edge case

The self-employed applicant with no collateral and a middling credit score:

- Self-employed, no collateral
- Income ≥ $50,000 (R3)
- Employed ≥ 12 months (R5, since there is no collateral)
- Credit ≥ 650, or credit ≥ 600 with income ≥ $80,000 (R4)
- Age ≥ 18

The tightest version is a **self-employed applicant with no collateral and a credit score between 600 and 649**. They need income ≥ $80,000 (R4's income requirement dominates R3's $50,000) and at least 12 months of self-employment. The minimum qualifying profile is:

- Age 18, income $80,000, credit 600, 12 months employed, self-employed, no collateral

Any step down on any value (age 17, income $79,999, credit 599, 11 months) fails. This is the only boundary where four rules bind at once.
