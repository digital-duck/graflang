Schedule 5 generators over 24 hours to meet hourly demand at minimum cost. Binary decisions: on[g,t] for each generator g and hour t. Constraints: demand coverage, generator min/max output when on, startup costs (paid when switching on), minimum up/down times, ramp limits. This is a hard MILP: 120 binary variables, tight coupling between periods. HiGHS solves LP and easy MILP well; SCIP adds cutting planes and branch-and-cut heuristics that close the gap faster on hard instances.
