STRIPS DOMAIN: logistics-lite
Objects: truck t1; packages p1, p2; locations A, B, C (any location reachable directly from any other — no roads to worry about)
Predicates: truck_at(T, L), pkg_at(P, L), pkg_in(P, T)
Actions (grounded, one per plan step):
  {"action": "drive", "truck": T, "from": L1, "to": L2}
    precondition: truck_at(T, L1)
    effect: truck no longer at L1, truck now at L2
  {"action": "load", "truck": T, "pkg": P, "loc": L}
    precondition: truck_at(T, L) AND pkg_at(P, L)
    effect: P no longer at L, P now in T
  {"action": "unload", "truck": T, "pkg": P, "loc": L}
    precondition: truck_at(T, L) AND pkg_in(P, T)
    effect: P no longer in T, P now at L
