Each player antes 1 chip and receives one private card (J, Q, or K). Player 1 acts first: Pass or Bet (1 chip). After Player 1 acts, Player 2 responds: Pass or Call/Bet. Showdown if needed — higher card wins the pot. Key tension: betting signals a strong card but also enables bluffing. Since neither player sees the other's card, optimal play requires a mixed (randomized) strategy to remain unexploitable.
